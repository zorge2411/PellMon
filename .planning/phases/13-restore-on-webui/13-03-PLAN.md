<objective>
Make the daemon core safe for an in-process restore: a module-level `restore_lock` that the Poller's RRD update and
`copy_db` take, a `conf.restore_done` flag that stops every "store" copy after a restore (so a ramdisk RRD never
overwrites the restored persistent one at shutdown), and the three new D-Bus methods the web page calls.

Purpose: Pitfalls 1 and 2 of the research (old RRD overwrites restored one; Poller writes during the swap).
Output: changes in src/Pellmonsrv/pellmonsrv.py plus extended tests. Independent of plans 13-01/13-02 (contract only).
</objective>
